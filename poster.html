PROMISSORY NOTE SYSTEM

Can't pay the full tuition yet? Pay at least 75%, file a Promissory Note for the remaining balance — the Registrar or Admin approves it, you're notified right away, and you can enroll & enlist your subjects.

HOW IT WORKS the 75% rule, step by step

  1. 1
    PAY AT LEAST 75% OF TUITION — the Accounting / Cashier records your payment with an Official Receipt 💵 recorded at the cashier and your dashboard progress bar updates automatically.
  2. 2
    FILE YOUR PROMISSORY NOTE — at 75% your dashboard turns PN ELIGIBLE and the remaining balance is auto-computed as your PN amount — add your reason, promise-to-pay date, e-signature, and the required ID photos (school ID front & back + guardian valid ID front & back).
  3. 3
    REGISTRAR / ADMIN REVIEW — the filing notifies the Registrar 🔔 new PN pending who checks your % paid, PN amount, reason and signature — then Approves ✓ or Rejects ✕ (with remarks; you may re-file). The Admin may also approve or reject any filing.
  4. 4
    YOU GET NOTIFIED RIGHT AWAY — ✅ PN APPROVED! lands in your notification bell dayon — approval automatically unlocks enrollment and the printable PN document.
  5. 5
    ENROLL & ENLIST SUBJECTS — tick your subjects up to max 24 units, then ✓ Confirm Enrollment — the Registrar receives your official enrollment summary. Fully-paid students skip the PN entirely.

FOUR ROLES, ONE SYSTEM

🎓

STUDENT

SELF-SERVICE
  • Tracks tuition progress & balance
  • Files the PN when ≥ 75% paid
  • Receives approval notifications
  • Enlists subjects & confirms enrollment
💵

ACCOUNTING / CASHIER

PAYMENTS
  • Records payments & prints official receipts
  • Live ledger of every student's % paid
  • Flags who is PN-eligible
  • Students auto-notified per payment
📋

REGISTRAR

APPROVAL
  • Reviews pending Promissory Notes
  • Approves ✓ / rejects ✕ with remarks
  • Prints official receipts & PN documents
  • Decisions unlock enrollment instantly
🛡

ADMIN

FULL POWER
  • Approves or rejects any filing
  • Control dashboard — stats & ledger
  • Records payments; prints PN & receipts
  • Notification log & demo data reset

🛡 KEY RULES BUILT INTO THE SYSTEM

  • 75% minimum payment before a PN can be filed.
  • Remaining balance is auto-computed (no manual math).
  • E-signature must match your full legal name.
  • Promise-to-pay date must be in the future.
  • Only one pending PN at a time, enrollment stays locked.
  • Rejected? Re-file a new PN with the remarks in mind.
  • Fully paid? No PN needed — enroll directly.
  • Max subject load: 24 units per semester.
